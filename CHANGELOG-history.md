# Free Hub — changelog

## Free Hub 1.6.0

- New "Menu font" setting at the bottom of the sidebar: shows the whole Free Hub window, including the options of every Free addon opened in it, in the font of your choice (each font previewed in the list), with a size offset (-3 to +3). Font previews and in-game elements keep their own fonts; an addon's options opened on their own keep Arial Narrow.


Newest first. Each release's notes come from `CHANGELOG.md` at the time it was published.

## Free Hub 1.5.6

- Free Minimap's product page: your quests and other addons' icons (Questie, RestedXP, TomTom…) on the region map.

## Free Hub 1.5.5

- Free Guild's product page: new gallery from in-game screenshots (roster, stats, rules, queue).

## Free Hub 1.5.4

- On a fresh install the window now opens in the middle of the screen (it could open off screen on smaller monitors).
- Product pages: Free Swing's class tag is shown whole, and the descriptions follow the latest versions of the Free addons (combo point styles and pet happiness in Free Unit Frames, Stats and Raids in Free Guild, free positioning in Free Minimap, Custom message in Free LFG).
- The sidebar no longer creates a hidden extra entry for Free Hub itself.
